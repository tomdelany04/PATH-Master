# Project Overview 

Heterogeneity of treatment effect (HTE) is the non-random variation in the magnitude or 
direction of a treatment effect across levels of a covariate, for a given clinical outcome.
In randomized controlled trials, HTE is often examined through sub-group analyses, contrasting effects in groups of patients defined ‘1 variable at a time’ e.g. male vs. female or old vs. young.

Predictive HTE analysis can compare patient-centered estimates of outcome risks between under intervention and under control, accounting for all relevant patient attributes at once.
Data from the Digitalis Investigation Group Trial, the International Stroke Trial, GUSTO - I trial and Vitamin D and Omega-3 clinical studies. These datasets will be analysed to explore relative treatment benefit of the interventions under study as a function of risk at baseline for death due to Cardiovascular disease or stroke.
